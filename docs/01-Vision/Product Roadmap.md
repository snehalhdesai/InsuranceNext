# Product Roadmap

> Strategic evolution roadmap for the InsuranceNext platform.

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Executive Leadership, Product Strategy & Platform Architecture

---

# Purpose

This roadmap describes the planned evolution of the InsuranceNext platform.

Unlike sprint plans or release schedules, this roadmap is strategic rather than tactical. It outlines the major capabilities the platform intends to deliver over time while remaining flexible enough to adapt to changing customer needs, technology advances, and market conditions.

The roadmap answers one question:

**"What platform are we building over the next five to ten years?"**

# Roadmap Philosophy

The roadmap is based on four principles:

- Deliver customer value incrementally.
- Build platform capabilities before platform complexity.
- Invest in reusable capabilities.
- Maintain architectural flexibility.

Each phase builds upon the previous one.

---

# Phase 1 — Platform Foundation

Objective:

Establish the technical and business foundations of InsuranceNext.

Key Deliverables:

- Identity and Access Management
- Organization Management
- Workspace Management
- User Management
- Investigation Framework
- Evidence Management
- Relationship Management
- Collections
- Search
- Audit Logging
- Notifications
- Initial API Platform

Outcome:

A secure, multi-tenant intelligence platform capable of supporting core investigative workflows.

---

# Phase 2 — Intelligence Platform

Objective:

Transform stored information into actionable intelligence.

Key Deliverables:

- Knowledge Graph
- Advanced Relationship Analysis
- Investigation Timelines
- Link Analysis
- AI Search
- Entity Resolution
- Graph Visualization
- Saved Intelligence Views
- Collaborative Investigations

Outcome:

Users move from collecting information to understanding information.

---

# Phase 3 — AI-Native Platform

Objective:

Embed AI throughout the platform as an intelligent collaborator.

Key Deliverables:

- Investigation Assistant
- Document Intelligence
- Automated Summaries
- Natural Language Search
- Recommendation Engine
- Insight Generation
- AI Workbench
- Prompt Library
- Explainable AI
- Human Approval Workflows

Outcome:

AI becomes an integrated assistant supporting every stage of investigative work.

---

# Phase 4 — Enterprise Platform

Objective:

Support enterprise-scale deployment and integration.

Key Deliverables:

- Workflow Engine
- Integration Hub
- Event Streaming
- Public APIs
- SDKs
- Partner Marketplace
- Advanced Reporting
- Multi-region Deployment
- High Availability
- Enterprise Administration

Outcome:

InsuranceNext becomes an extensible enterprise platform that integrates seamlessly with existing technology ecosystems.

---

# Phase 5 — Intelligence Ecosystem

Objective:

Expand beyond investigations into a comprehensive insurance intelligence platform.

Potential Capability Areas:

- Claims Intelligence
- Fraud Intelligence
- Underwriting Intelligence
- Risk Intelligence
- Compliance Intelligence
- Customer Intelligence
- Regulatory Intelligence
- Enterprise Knowledge Management
- Predictive Analytics
- Industry Benchmarking

Outcome:

InsuranceNext evolves into the central intelligence platform for insurance organizations.

---

# Cross-Phase Capabilities

The following capabilities evolve continuously across every phase:

- Security
- Performance
- Scalability
- Accessibility
- Compliance
- AI Governance
- User Experience
- Observability
- Developer Experience
- Documentation

These are ongoing investments rather than discrete projects.

---

# Delivery Principles

Roadmap execution should:

- Deliver incremental value.
- Minimize disruption.
- Preserve backward compatibility where practical.
- Validate assumptions through customer feedback.
- Maintain architectural integrity.

Features should only advance to production when they align with the Product Principles and Business Goals.

---

# Roadmap Governance

The roadmap is owned by Product Strategy in partnership with Platform Architecture and Executive Leadership.

It should be reviewed:

- Quarterly for prioritization.
- Annually for strategic alignment.
- Following significant market or regulatory changes.

Major roadmap changes should be documented with supporting rationale.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Over time, InsuranceNext aims to become:

- The intelligence operating system for insurers.
- A platform for enterprise AI collaboration.
- A trusted repository of organizational knowledge.
- A foundation for partner ecosystems and industry innovation.

The roadmap should continue evolving while remaining faithful to the long-term vision established in `Vision.md`.
