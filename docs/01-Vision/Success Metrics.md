# Success Metrics

> Defining the measurable outcomes used to evaluate the success of the InsuranceNext platform.

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Executive Leadership, Product Strategy & Customer Success

---

# Purpose

This document defines the strategic success metrics for InsuranceNext.

These metrics provide a framework for evaluating whether the platform is delivering meaningful business value to customers and achieving its long-term vision.

The purpose of these metrics is not to measure engineering activity, but to measure customer outcomes.

# Measurement Philosophy

InsuranceNext measures success based on outcomes rather than outputs.

We do not measure success by:

- Number of features delivered
- Lines of code written
- Number of deployments
- Number of AI models implemented

Instead, we measure improvements experienced by customers.

---

# Strategic Success Areas

## 1. Investigation Efficiency

Objective:

Reduce the effort required to complete investigations.

Example Metrics:

- Average investigation completion time
- Time spent locating evidence
- Time spent preparing reports
- Average investigation workload per investigator

Target Direction:

Decrease

---

## 2. Decision Quality

Objective:

Enable better business decisions.

Example Metrics:

- Reduction in investigation rework
- Reduction in incorrect claim decisions
- Increase in evidence-backed decisions
- Increase in AI-assisted recommendations accepted by users

Target Direction:

Improve

---

## 3. Fraud Detection

Objective:

Improve fraud identification capability.

Example Metrics:

- Fraud detection rate
- Fraud detection accuracy
- Time to identify suspicious relationships
- Percentage of high-risk investigations identified early

Target Direction:

Increase

---

## 4. User Productivity

Objective:

Reduce manual effort.

Example Metrics:

- Click reduction for common workflows
- Manual data entry reduction
- Automated document processing rate
- AI-assisted workflow adoption

Target Direction:

Increase productivity while reducing repetitive work.

---

## 5. Knowledge Discovery

Objective:

Improve access to organizational knowledge.

Example Metrics:

- Average search time
- Search success rate
- Relationship discovery rate
- Reuse of previous investigations
- Knowledge graph utilization

Target Direction:

Increase

---

## 6. Collaboration

Objective:

Improve collaboration across teams.

Example Metrics:

- Shared investigations
- Multi-user investigations
- Cross-department participation
- Comment and annotation activity

Target Direction:

Increase

---

## 7. Platform Reliability

Objective:

Provide enterprise-grade reliability.

Example Metrics:

- Platform uptime
- API availability
- Mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Incident frequency

Target Direction:

99.9% availability or higher, subject to deployment model and service-level objectives.

---

## 8. Security & Compliance

Objective:

Maintain customer trust.

Example Metrics:

- Security incidents
- Unauthorized access attempts blocked
- Audit completeness
- Compliance reporting accuracy

Target Direction:

Maintain strong security posture while minimizing operational disruption.

---

## 9. AI Effectiveness

Objective:

Deliver trustworthy AI assistance.

Example Metrics:

- AI recommendation acceptance rate
- User feedback score
- Explanation usefulness
- Reduction in manual analysis effort
- Time saved using AI assistance

Target Direction:

Increase user confidence and adoption without reducing transparency.

---

## 10. Customer Value

Objective:

Deliver measurable business outcomes.

Example Metrics:

- Customer satisfaction (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Customer retention
- Expansion within existing customers
- Time to customer value

Target Direction:

Increase long-term customer success.

---

# Engineering Health Indicators

Although customer outcomes remain the primary focus, engineering health should also be monitored.

Examples include:

- Deployment frequency
- Change failure rate
- Lead time for changes
- Automated test coverage
- Technical debt backlog

These indicators support sustainable delivery but are not considered product success metrics.

---

# Review Process

Success metrics should be reviewed:

- Quarterly by Product Management.
- Biannually by Executive Leadership.
- Continuously through operational dashboards where data is available.

Metrics should evolve as the platform matures, while maintaining alignment with the long-term vision.

---

# Guiding Principles

Success metrics should be:

- Meaningful
- Actionable
- Measurable
- Customer-centric
- Transparent
- Aligned with strategic business goals
